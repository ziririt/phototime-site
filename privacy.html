Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Photo Time ("the app") is made by ezlong. This policy explains what the app uses and why. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

What the app uses

  • Your photos (only the albums or photos you choose) are read from your device's photo library and shown on screen. They are never uploaded or copied off your device.
  • Date and place of each photo are read from the photo itself. To turn a photo's location into a place name, the rounded coordinates are sent to your device's built-in place-name service (Apple on iPhone and iPad, Google on Android). The photo itself is not sent. You can turn place names off in Settings.
  • Your location (latitude and longitude, only if you allow it) is sent to our weather server to get the weather where you are. It is not stored on our server and is not linked to you.
  • Settings such as your frame, albums and music choices are saved only on your device.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or account.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No contacts or files. Photos you did not choose are not shown, and no photo is ever uploaded.

Services the app connects to

  • Weather: Visual Crossing, through our server on Cloudflare.
  • Place names for the weather: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  • Place names for your photos: Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android), through the device's built-in service.
  • Music and background scenery: Cloudflare R2 and Google Firebase Storage (scenery photos from Pexels and Wikimedia Commons).
  • These services receive only what is needed to answer each request.

Children

  • The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
